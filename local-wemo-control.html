Local Wemo control without a cloud account

Local Control for Wemo is an Android app for compatible legacy Wemo smart plugs, switches, and dimmers on your home Wi-Fi or LAN.

Independent local controller for compatible legacy Wemo devices. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Belkin.

What It Does

Local discovery

Find compatible Wemo devices on the same Wi-Fi or wired LAN using local UPnP discovery.

Local control

Turn supported plugs and switches on or off, and adjust supported dimmers, using local device requests.

No cloud account

The app does not require a Wemo or Belkin account. It is focused on home-network control.

Device organization

Use rooms, favorites, saved order, widgets, and device detail screens to keep older Wemo devices usable.

Who It Helps

This app is for people who already have compatible legacy Wemo devices installed at home and want a simple local controller. It is especially useful when a device still works on the local network but cloud or official app behavior is unreliable or unavailable.

It is not a cloud bridge, not an outside-home remote access service, and not an official replacement app from Belkin.

Requirements

  • An Android phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi or LAN as the Wemo devices.
  • Compatible legacy Wemo devices that expose local UPnP/SOAP control.
  • Local network settings that allow multicast discovery. If discovery misses a device, the app can try a manual local IP address.

Want Matter Support?

Use a Raspberry Pi as a local WeMo-to-Matter bridge to add compatible legacy Wemo devices to Apple Home, Google Home, or another Matter controller.

Normal users should use the latest WeMo to Matter Bridge for Raspberry Pi release. Raspberry Pi 4 or CM4 is the recommended target. Raspberry Pi 5 or CM5 may be available or experimental depending on the current release.

Download the correct openwemo-rpi*-...-sysupgrade.img.gz image from the release page and follow its flashing and pairing instructions. The QR code pairs the bridge once; individual Wemo devices appear as bridged Matter endpoints.

This bridge is a separate local project and does not claim Matter certification.

FAQ

Does it require the Wemo cloud?

No. The app is designed for local home-network control.

Can it control devices when I am away from home?

No. Your phone must be on the same local Wi-Fi or LAN as the devices.

Does it support every Wemo product?

No. It targets compatible legacy Wemo devices that expose local UPnP/SOAP control. Unsupported models may not appear or may show limited functionality.

Does it set up new Wemo devices?

Setup is planned, but currently disabled while compatibility and credential handling continue testing. The current focus is controlling devices already on your home network.

Can I add Wemo devices to Apple Home or Google Home with Matter?

Use the separate WeMo to Matter Bridge for Raspberry Pi release. The bridge runs locally and exposes compatible LAN Wemo devices as bridged Matter endpoints.

What device information leaves my phone?

Local Wemo names, IP addresses, rooms, and troubleshooting details stay on the phone unless you choose to share diagnostics. The app uses Google Mobile Ads, so advertising-related data is handled under Google policies.
